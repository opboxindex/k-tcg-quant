Transparency

Data corrections & change history

When a number on this site turns out to be wrong, it gets fixed and recorded here — date, what was wrong, and what changed. Automated collection also breaks sometimes; outages that affected published data are listed too. Entries below are drawn from the project's actual commit history. Found something wrong that isn't listed? Email kimtt1107@gmail.com.

  • 2026-11-05

    CorrectionPSA 10 lowest-listing links: one eBay listing could be attached to printings in two sets. PRB-01 Shanks Manga showed a 2022 Romance Dawn (OP-01) slab ($1,756 ask); PRB-01 Ace Manga showed a 2023 listing whose eBay set field reads Paramount War, OP-02’s set name ($850); EB-05 Nami OP01-016 SP showed a 2023 OP05 listing ($295). A listing now has to match the card’s own printing (set code, set name or year), and a listing two cards share is shown on neither unless its title settles which one. 16 cards’ listing links are hidden until a matching listing is found.

  • 2026-11-05

    CorrectionJapanese NM collection skipped the first product on every retail list page (the page menu was read as a card), so OP06-118 Zoro AA, OP10-118 Luffy parallel, EB01-061 Bonkurei AA and PRB-02’s OP06-119 Sanji manga showed no NM price. Fixed; all 23 sets were re-read on 2026-11-05.

  • 2026-11-05

    CorrectionHand-written counts that had gone stale are now generated from the data on every build. The home page said 22 sets and 14 articles (23 and 16); the Southeast Asia article named OP-17 (released August 22) as the next set and said the comparison table ranks 21 boxes (23 sets); the Japanese-vs-English article linked “all 20 pairs” (19); the packs article’s “18 sets with a sold median” counted only sets with a vs-MSRP multiple (19 have a median), and now says what it counts. The AI summary’s 7-day One Piece auction total included a partially collected day; it now uses complete days only, like the auction page.

  • 2026-11-05

    CorrectionThe OP-16 one-month report and the six-month Japanese-vs-English report showed July values from different bases in one table without labels: a third-party weekly market value, weekly eBay sold medians and an eBay asking-price middle (the Japanese OP-16 $150 on July 16 was an asking price, not a sale). Each column or value now carries its basis; the recorded values are unchanged. The articles list called that July value “now $150”. The OP-16 report pointed to OP-14 and OP-15 launch paths on the OP-17 page that no longer exist; removed.

  • 2026-11-05

    CopyThe About page listed weekly ungraded market values as a current box-price source; that series stopped in July 2026 and is now dated. The AI summary file said “nothing is estimated” although two dated reports use those market values; removed.

  • 2026-11-02

    CorrectionEB-05 moved from a pre-release page to a full set page. Card fixes at launch: EB05-016 and EB05-006 relabelled as SP with Bandai’s SP images; the foil-stamped Nami EB05-055’s Japanese NM price is hidden until its variant is confirmed. Box series: a fallback that kept a weekly point with its outliers when too few clean sales remained was removed, leaving four points blank (OP-07 Japanese 9/27 $134; OP-09 Japanese 3/29–4/12).

  • 2026-10-28

    OutageDaily eBay listing and exchange-rate collection was blocked for 3 days (2026-10-26 to 10-28): a pre-collection check compared the published AI files with a grading ledger another job had just updated, and stopped each run before collecting. That check now runs after the build. Listing-based figures and exchange rates were not refreshed on those days.

  • 2026-10-21

    CorrectionOP-12’s Marshall D. Teach SP (OP09-093) Gold and Silver had their Japanese NM prices swapped: Silver showed ¥148,000 and Gold ¥99,800. Matching now settles variants that the name decides (gold, silver, red, super, wanted poster) before matching by closeness to the previous price; re-running changed only these two cards.

  • 2026-10-15

    CorrectionOP-07’s Japanese 4-week change showed −40.3%: three of the six sales behind its mid-September medians were one-off high prices. Single sales far from the median of both the prior and the following 90 days are now left out of box medians (still counted in sales volume; the sales record is unchanged; rule on the methodology page). OP-07 moved to 0.0%; 33 sales across all series were left out at the time.

  • 2026-10-01

    CorrectionEvery box sale date had been stored one day earlier than eBay shows it since collection began on 2026-07-22 (the displayed date was converted through UTC). All sale dates were moved forward one day; medians are unchanged, their dates moved by a day, and month-end sales moved to the next month’s bucket.

  • 2026-09-24

    CorrectionGundam Card Game “Eternal Nexus EB01” boxes (9 sales, ~$100–140) had entered the EB-01 sold ledger because the two games share the EB01 code. They pulled the English EB-01 sold median from about $900 to $140 on the set page, its meta description, the CSV and the AI files. Moved to the ledger’s excluded list (never deleted); the ingest now rejects other-game titles and the build guard fails if any remain. English EB-01 restored to $901.

  • 2026-09-24

    CorrectionThe home summary table, the 4-week ticker and box-prices.html showed every set’s last sold price under one page date, although four sets had not sold in 4–11 weeks. Every row and chip now carries its own last-sale date and weekly sample size; sets with no sale in 28 days are dimmed and their 4-week change is left blank. The home FAQ said the 4-week figures were “since tracking began” — corrected.

  • 2026-09-24

    CorrectionCard pages computed “worth Nx a sealed box” against a third-party weekly series that stopped in July, so the same box showed $148 on a card page and $129 on its set page. Card pages now use the same eBay sold median as the set page, dated, and omit the multiple when the last sale is older than 28 days. PSA 10 sold medians older than 35 days no longer produce a “premium” sentence on card pages and are dated on set pages; price-history checkpoints convert at each date’s exchange rate.

  • 2026-09-24

    CorrectionBox charts: the monthly tab hatched the whole current month as “no sales” because monthly points are dated to the 1st; the gap is now measured from the real last sale. The change badge compared the last point with the first visible point and flipped sign between tabs; it now uses one basis (vs 28 days earlier, the same as the ticker). The listings-on-sale panel drew JP and EN in the same grey; each edition now keeps its colour.

  • 2026-09-23

    CopyExclusion filter disclosed next to the numbers: asking-price figures count only sellers that passed verification (China/Hong Kong sellers excluded for reseal and counterfeit risk — 348 of 709 collected box listings on 2026-09-23). The excluded floor price is shown without a link. Methodology gained an “exclusions” section with the criteria and the public blocklist.

  • 2026-09-22

    CorrectionPSA 10 sold medians up to 85 days old were presented as updated today on 18 cards; they now carry their observation month and are dimmed after 35 days, and no premium multiple is computed from them. Unsealed, resealed and pack-missing boxes were reaching the “cheapest listing” slot on the home deals; excluded.
